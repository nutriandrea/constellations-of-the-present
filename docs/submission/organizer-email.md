# ISEA2026 Organizer Email — Draft

**To:** ISEA2026 Digital Arts Student Competition organizers (submit at the email address listed in the Call for Participation / competition brief)
**Subject:** Submission format question — interactive web artwork "Constellations of the Present"

---

Dear ISEA2026 organizers,

My name is Andrea Cacioppo, a student at Politecnico di Milano. I am preparing a submission for the Digital Arts Student Competition around the theme *ELYAH — Constellating Place, Data, Identity*, and I would like to confirm the acceptable formats for the artwork before I finalize my application package.

My proposed work, *Constellations of the Present*, is an interactive web artwork: a public single-page site in which each visitor's presence is sensed on-device, reduced to a non-reversible "moment-hash," and placed as a star in a constellation shared in real time by everyone online at that moment. Nothing raw ever leaves the device — the piece operates under a strict privacy-by-design constraint. A short demo video and a public URL are available.

In the Call for Participation, "other digital formats" are listed as acceptable alongside traditional media. Since my work is inherently interactive and browser-based, I wanted to confirm two things:

1. Is an interactive web artwork delivered as a public URL an accepted submission format?
2. Would you require any additional materials for it, such as the source repository, a demo video, or a documentation file?

I would be happy to provide a demo, the URL, the code repository, and any technical documentation needed. My submission deadline target is the competition deadline, and I want to ensure everything is in order ahead of time.

Thank you very much for your time.
