# Artist Bio (≤ 75 words)

Andrea Cacioppo is an Italian designer and creative developer based in Milan, currently studying at Politecnico di Milano. His practice sits at the intersection of interaction design, creative code, and privacy-by-design, exploring how networked systems can create intimacy and collectivity without surveillance. His works are meant to be experienced live, in the present tense, leaving nothing behind. Constellations of the Present is his first networked artwork.
