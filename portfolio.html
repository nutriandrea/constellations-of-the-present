01 / Concept

The sky reads how you feel.

The same stars for everyone, everywhere. Constellations of the Present rebuilds the sky for the network age: a collective surface that exists only in the living present, built from moments instead of profiles.

Sensing happens entirely on your device. The camera watches nothing — it maps tiny facial clues into six emotional readings, then drops them into the sky as an anonymous star. Emotion becomes colour; your expression, for one split second, is shared by everyone watching the same sky. What leaves the device is only a livestream of predictions — never the face, never the image.

Six readings, one gradient

Joy

A wide smile and squinted eyes — the brightest light in the sky.

smile high · squint high · frown low

Surprise

An open jaw and raised brows — a supernova, brief and white-hot.

jaw high · eye widen high · brow up med

Anger

Lowered brows, pressed lips — a red star burning against the void.

brow down high · lips med · frown med

Sadness

A frown and raised inner brows — a cool, distant light.

frown med · brow up med · smile low

Calm

A gentle, soft smile — the quiet light most skies are made of.

soft smile high · jaw low · frown low

Neutral

Nothing crosses the threshold — a steady, neutral star.

confidence low · threshold under

The six readings are averaged across the crowd: stars lean toward the dominant emotion, mingling into a shared emotional gradient that softly tints the whole sky.

02 / Privacy

Nothing raw is transmitted, stored or logged.

On-device sensing

Face tracking runs entirely in the browser and is discarded frame by frame. No image or video ever leaves the device.

Moment-hash

Quantised expression and engagement values are hashed with a random nonce. The result is anonymous and non-reversible.

Opt-in audio only

The microphone feeds only a live audio level into an ambient drone. It never records, never transmits, never leaves the browser.
